meet the maker

Hi, I'm Keisha.

Every pin on this site started the same way — a doodle in the margin of a notebook, a color combination that wouldn't leave my head, a scrap of felt that seemed too pretty to throw away. Pins by Keisha grew out of that habit of noticing.

What began as gifts for friends turned into a studio table covered in enamel, thread, and paint, where every single piece is still cut, colored, and packaged by hand — mine.

I don't believe creativity needs to be perfect to be meaningful. It just needs to be made with care. That's the only promise behind every order: it was made just for you, by someone who genuinely loves making it.
